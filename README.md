# Lens Pro — Privacy & Support

Lens Pro is a Mac app for recording tutorials and demos and preparing smaller media files to share.

- [iCloud storage full? Find large photos and videos on Mac — practical guide and demo](https://lens-pro-app.github.io/Lens-privacy/icloud-photos-storage-mac.html)
- [Read the privacy policy](https://lens-pro-app.github.io/Lens-privacy/privacy-policy.html)
- [Get help and contact support](https://github.com/Lens-Pro-App/Lens-privacy/blob/main/SUPPORT.md#contact-us)

## Privacy at a glance

- Recording and compression run on your Mac.
- Lens has no account system, advertising, or automatic analytics.
- Photos access is optional. Apple may download an original from iCloud Photos when you request it.
- Send Feedback opens a mail draft with support information and redacted diagnostic logs. You review it and decide whether to send it.
- Purchases use Apple's StoreKit and App Store services.

See the full policy for permissions, local storage, optional support correspondence, and Apple's services.

## About Lens Pro

Version 1.2.0 requires macOS 14.6 or later and includes screen, window, and region recording; microphone and system audio; an optional webcam overlay; video compression and trimming; and optional Photos library tools.
